# Store listing

**Title:** Webtoon Clone

**Short description (80 characters):** A fast, dark reader for MangaDex with offline history and new chapter alerts.

**Full description:**

Read manga and webtoons from MangaDex in a clean, dark app.

- Vertical strip reading for webtoons, and paged reading left to right or right to left for manga. The app picks a mode from the series' tags and saves your choice per series.
- Pinch and double-tap zoom, tap zones, volume-key paging, and auto-scroll.
- Search by title, tag, status, demographic, original language, and year. Include or exclude tags.
- Subscribe to series and get a notification when a new chapter appears. Set quiet hours.
- Reading lists: Reading, Plan to Read, Completed, Dropped.
- Chapters in 22 languages, grouped by volume, with scanlation group credits and a preferred group per series.
- Back up your library and settings to a file, and restore them on another device.
- Open mangadex.org links directly in the app. Continue Reading widget and launcher shortcuts.

The app shows content from MangaDex. It is not affiliated with MangaDex.

**Category:** Comics
**Content rating:** Mature 17+ (MangaDex content tags include violence and sexual themes)
**Privacy:** No account. Library data stays on the device. Page loads are reported to MangaDex@Home, which can be switched off in Settings. Crash reports stay on the device unless you share them.
